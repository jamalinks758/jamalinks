The Rider

BORN IN SAINT LUCIA.
BUILT FOR THE CLIMB.

Kiefer Julien grew up on the hilly roads of Saint Lucia — the kind of terrain that doesn't ask permission before it breaks you. The island's geography gave him his engine. Those long climbs out of Soufrière, the hairpins above Marigot Bay, the switchbacks heading into the hills above Castries — they are the same roads where the region's best riders have been forged for generations.

By his mid-twenties, Julien had translated that foundation into a career that makes him the most accomplished road cyclist Saint Lucia has ever produced: 22 race wins, 3 national titles, a CONCACAF Championship, and a UCI Pan-American ranking that has drawn attention from Continental-level teams in the Americas and Europe alike. His specialism — aggressive, early-race climbs and long breakaway riding — suits his physiology exactly. He doesn't wait for the sprint.

What sets Kiefer apart commercially is not just the results. It is the platform he represents — the first Caribbean climber with the profile, the narrative, and the performance data to carry a brand beyond the island and into the global cycling conversation.
