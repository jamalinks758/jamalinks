The Player

Built in the Caribbean.
Ready for the World.

Marcus Felix grew up in Vieux-Fort, Saint Lucia, playing barefoot on concrete pitches under lights that cut out without warning. That environment taught him something no academy can — how to be effective under pressure, with nothing guaranteed.

At 23, he has accumulated what most Caribbean footballers take a decade to achieve: a regional championship, a national team calling, a reputation as the most unpredictable striker in the CFU. His low centre of gravity, technical finishing, and reading of defensive lines are tracked by scouts across three leagues.

What sets Marcus apart is not just what he produces on the pitch — it is what he represents off it. A clean professional image, a growing social following, and a story the Caribbean diaspora connects with immediately. That combination makes him investable.
