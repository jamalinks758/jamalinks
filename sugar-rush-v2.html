Transporting Your Cake

Follow these rules to protect your masterpiece on the journey home.

NEVER place the cake on a car seat — they are slanted and the cake will slide, causing damage.

ALWAYS place the cake flat on the vehicle floor — it must remain level throughout the journey.

KEEP IT COOL: Air conditioning on at all times. Never leave a cake in a parked vehicle.
