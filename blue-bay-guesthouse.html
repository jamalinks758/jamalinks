Local Insider Guide

What we tell every guest.

🏖️ Best Beaches

Reduit Beach for swimming, Anse Chastanet for snorkeling and reef access.

🍽️ Best Local Food

Friday night fish fry in Anse La Raye — worth planning your week around.

🚕 Taxi Tips

Agree the fare before getting in; we can call a trusted driver for you.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Police 999, Ambulance 911, and our front desk is always reachable on WhatsApp.

🎁 Souvenirs

Castries Market for crafts, skip the resort gift shops.

☔ Rainy Day Ideas

Diamond Botanical Gardens has covered paths and mineral baths.

☕ Coffee Shops

Coal Pot Café, a 4-minute walk, does the best local roast in the area.

🤝 Island Etiquette

A greeting before any request goes a long way here — "good morning" first, question second.
