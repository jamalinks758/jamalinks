The Artist

A passion born from
a Brixton street market.

It started with a small set of unbranded piping tips — picked up from a market stall in Brixton, London. One leaf tip from that set has featured in every vintage cake Dominique has ever made. She guards it fiercely to this day.

Her niche found her by accident. After making her first vintage cake for a baby shower celebration with just 900 followers, the requests changed overnight. Everyone wanted the vintage style. That single cake became the turning point that shaped everything that followed.

Based in Chandler, Brisbane, Dominique now creates handcrafted buttercream cakes for families, events, and celebrations across Queensland. Her work — defined by Victorian Lambeth piping, sculpted ruffles, and intricate florals — has earned over 111,000 followers who share her belief that a cake should be more than just a cake.

She is grateful for every customer who has supported her from the very beginning, and for every family that trusts her with their most important celebrations.
