My Framework

The CLARITY Method

Every client I work with moves through the same seven-phase framework — adapted entirely to her life, her timeline, and her version of success. This is not a formula. It's a compass.

C
Confront the Current
We name what's really going on — without judgement, without softening. Honest baseline assessment of where you are vs. where you want to be.
L
Locate Your Values
Identify the non-negotiables that must be present in your life for it to feel like yours. Most confusion disappears when your values are clear.
A
Audit the Noise
Separate your voice from the external expectations — family, culture, career pressure — that have been steering your decisions for years.
R
Redefine Success
Build your personal definition of a well-lived life. Not a borrowed version — yours. This phase changes how everything else feels.
I
Ignite the Vision
From values and definition, we craft a concrete, emotionally real vision of where you're going. Specific, alive, and actually motivating.
T
Take Structured Action
Vision without roadmap is just daydreaming. We build the plan — with milestones, accountability, and built-in flexibility for real life.
Y
You, Sustained
The goal isn't one breakthrough. It's a new operating system. We close with practices, habits, and anchors that keep you moving — long after our last session.

About Nadine
I've been where you are.

At 34, I left a stable government job, a relationship everyone told me was "the right one," and the carefully constructed version of my life that had nothing to do with me. I had all the things and none of the clarity.

Over the two years that followed — which were the hardest and most freeing of my life — I discovered what it actually meant to live from intention rather than expectation. That experience became my coaching practice.

I trained through an ICF-accredited programme, logged over 500 hours of professional coaching, and became certified in NLP and mindset coaching. But what matters more than any credential is this: I coach from lived experience, not theory.

Today I work exclusively with women navigating the kind of pivots that feel too big to discuss with friends and too personal for a career counsellor. Women who are ready — but need the space and the structure to become clear.

Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How do I know if life coaching is right for me?
Coaching is right for you if you're mentally well, functioning, but feeling stuck — not because of a clinical condition, but because of uncertainty, competing priorities, or lack of clarity. If you feel like you're capable of more but can't seem to make the move, that's exactly who I work with. Coaching is not therapy. If you're navigating trauma or a mental health condition, I'll refer you to the appropriate support.
What does a typical coaching session look like?
Sessions are 60–90 minutes and held via WhatsApp Voice, Zoom, or in person in Saint Lucia. I start each session by reviewing your wins and blockers from the previous week, then we work through the live topic — decision, obstacle, pattern — using questions, frameworks, and exercises. You leave every session with specific actions to take before we meet again.
How quickly will I see results?
Most clients feel a meaningful shift within the first two sessions — not because I give answers, but because the right questions cut through what you've been avoiding. Sustained results come from the full programme. Three of my clients made life-changing decisions — career moves, relationship decisions, relocations — within 60 days of completing the Clarity Journey programme.
Do you work with clients outside Saint Lucia?
Yes — the majority of my clients are in the Caribbean diaspora, currently based in the UK, USA, and Canada. Sessions via WhatsApp Voice or Zoom work seamlessly across time zones. I schedule around Caribbean Standard Time (AST) and accommodate UK and US East Coast schedules with evening sessions on request.
What is your cancellation policy?
Sessions can be rescheduled with 24 hours' notice at no penalty. Late cancellations (under 12 hours) or no-shows are forfeited. I hold a limited number of clients at a time to give each woman my full attention — which is why this policy exists: your spot is held for you, and I ask you to respect that commitment as you would your own time.
How do payments work?
Payment is in USD via PayPal invoice. Single sessions are paid in full before the session. Programme packages require a 50% deposit to secure your place, with the remaining 50% due before session 4. Payment plans (2 instalments) are available for both the Clarity Journey and Deep Transformation packages — ask me about this when you reach out.
