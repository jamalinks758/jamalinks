Local Experiences

The island, beyond the pool deck.

Snorkeling

Reef Brigade takes small groups out to Anse Chastanet's reef, 20 minutes south.

Rainforest Adventures

Zip-lining and canopy walks through the interior rainforest, half-day trips.

Sulphur Springs

The world's only drive-in volcano, with mud baths a short drive from the villa.

Island Sailing

Half-day catamaran runs along the west coast, past the Pitons.

Fishing Trips

Half or full-day deep-sea charters out of Rodney Bay Marina.

Waterfalls

Toraille and Diamond Falls are both under 40 minutes from the villa.

Local Cuisine

We'll point you to the Friday night Anse La Raye fish fry — worth the trip.

Sunset Cruises

Evening catamaran runs timed for the golden hour over the Pitons.
