Clinical Specialties

Expert Care Across the Full
Spectrum of Digestive Health

From the most common digestive complaints to complex inflammatory conditions and cancer screening — Dr. Holder provides the full breadth of consultant gastroenterology care, without the need to travel abroad.

Diagnostic Endoscopy

Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy (gastroscopy) for the investigation of swallowing problems, stomach pain, reflux, ulcers, and unexplained bleeding. Performed under sedation with same-day results.

Colonoscopy & Bowel Screening

Colonoscopy for bowel cancer screening, polyp detection and removal, investigation of rectal bleeding, and the diagnosis and monitoring of inflammatory bowel conditions.

Liver Disease

Assessment and management of fatty liver disease, hepatitis B and C, cirrhosis, abnormal liver function tests, and alcohol-related liver conditions. Including fibroscan assessment.

Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Diagnosis and ongoing management of Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis — including medication optimisation, monitoring, and flare management — for patients across Saint Lucia and the Eastern Caribbean.

Reflux & Upper GI Disorders

Management of acid reflux (GERD), Barrett's oesophagus, hiatus hernia, H. pylori infection, and gastroparesis — from diagnosis through to long-term treatment planning.

GI Cancer Screening

Structured colorectal cancer screening programmes for at-risk individuals, including those with family history, Lynch syndrome, or prior polyp removal. Personalised screening intervals set and managed.

About Dr. Holder

Sixteen Years.
One Commitment:
Caribbean Patients Deserve Better.

Marcus Holder grew up in Castries and watched his own family navigate a healthcare system where specialist care often meant a flight to Barbados or Trinidad — or going without it entirely. He trained as a physician with the specific intention of returning. That decision has defined everything since.

After completing his medical degree and gastroenterology training in the United Kingdom, including Fellowship of the Royal College of Physicians in London, Dr. Holder returned to Saint Lucia to establish the island's most comprehensive private gastroenterology practice. Over sixteen years and more than 3,200 patients, he has built a practice that brings consultant-level care to the Caribbean without compromise.

His approach is unhurried, direct, and thorough. He explains what he finds in plain terms, involves patients fully in treatment decisions, and remains accessible throughout the process. That is a standard he believes every Caribbean patient deserves — and the standard he delivers, without exception.

Common Questions

Before You
Reach Out

Anything else — ask via WhatsApp and Dr. Holder's team will respond personally.

Do I need a referral from my GP to see Dr. Holder?
No. Dr. Holder accepts both self-referred patients and those referred by their GP or family physician. You can contact his team directly on WhatsApp to arrange a consultation at any time.
How soon can I be seen?
Initial consultations are typically available within a few days of enquiry. For urgent clinical concerns — such as significant rectal bleeding or unexplained weight loss — appointments are prioritised and can often be arranged the same week.
Is my consultation completely confidential?
Yes. All consultations with Dr. Holder are governed by full medical confidentiality. Your health information is never shared without your explicit consent. This applies from the first message you send.
What does a colonoscopy or gastroscopy involve?
Both procedures are performed under sedation, so you will be comfortable throughout and will not feel any pain. A gastroscopy (upper endoscopy) takes approximately 15–20 minutes. A colonoscopy typically takes 20–40 minutes depending on findings. You will need someone to accompany you home afterwards, and results are discussed with you on the same day where possible.
When should someone be considered for bowel cancer screening?
Dr. Holder recommends that all patients aged 45 and over discuss bowel cancer screening — even without symptoms. Those with a family history of colorectal cancer, prior polyp removal, or diagnosed conditions such as inflammatory bowel disease should begin screening earlier. A consultation will establish the appropriate interval and method for your personal risk profile.
