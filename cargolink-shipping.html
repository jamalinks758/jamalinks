Packaging Resource Center

Pack it right the first time.

🛢️
How to Pack a Barrel

Heaviest items at the bottom, soft goods on top, fill gaps to prevent shifting during transit.

🎁
Protecting Fragile Items

Wrap individually, cushion on all sides, and mark the barrel or box clearly as fragile.

🏷️
Labeling Shipments

Full recipient name, phone number and destination address, visible on two sides.

⚖️
Weight Distribution

Even weight prevents damage and keeps handling fees predictable — no single side overloaded.

📋
Recommended Materials

Bubble wrap, packing paper, and sturdy tape — avoid loose newspaper for anything fragile.

🛃
Reducing Customs Delays

List contents accurately and keep receipts for higher-value electronics or appliances.
