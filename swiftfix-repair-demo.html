Common Questions

Questions Customers
Always Ask

Do you fix all phone brands?
Yes — we repair iPhone, Samsung, Huawei, Oppo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Tecno and most other Android brands. Message us your model if you're unsure.
How long do most repairs take?
Screen replacements typically take 30–60 minutes. Battery replacements are 20–45 minutes. Laptops and complex repairs can take 1–4 hours. We always give you an estimated time before you drop off.
What if you can't fix my device?
If we can't repair it, you don't pay anything. No diagnostic fee, no assessment charge — nothing. That's our no fix, no fee promise and it's unconditional.
Are your parts genuine / original?
We use OEM-equivalent or genuine parts depending on your device. We'll always tell you which we're using and why. We never use low-grade parts that compromise your device.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes — message us on WhatsApp with your device model and the issue. We'll give you a confirmed price range in minutes so there are no surprises when you arrive.
