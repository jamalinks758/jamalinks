Safety First

What we actually do to keep you safe.

  • ✓ Daily equipment inspections before first launch
  • ✓ Certified instructors on every activity
  • ✓ Life jackets included and required, no exceptions
  • ✓ Continuous weather and sea condition monitoring
  • ✓ Professional safety briefing before every departure
  • ✓ Staff trained in emergency response and first aid
  • ✓ Activities matched to your swimming ability and experience
  • ✓ Liability coverage on all guided experiences

What to Bring

  • Swimwear
  • Sunscreen (reef-safe)
  • Towel
  • Change of clothes
  • Water bottle
  • Sunglasses (strapped)
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Closed-toe water shoes

Medical conditions or medications that affect balance or breathing? Let us know when booking — we'll advise on the safest activity for you.
