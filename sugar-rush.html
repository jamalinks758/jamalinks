Transporting Your Cake

Please follow these rules to keep your masterpiece safe on the way home.

NEVER place the cake on a car seat. Seats are slanted — the cake can slide backwards off the board in the box, causing damage.

ALWAYS place the cake flat on the floor of your vehicle — it must remain level throughout the entire journey.

KEEP IT COOL: Air conditioning on at all times. Never leave a cake in a parked car in Barbados heat.
