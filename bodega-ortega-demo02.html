the ortega story

Built In Memory.
Built For Community.

None of the Ortegas came up in the food world. What they had was Puerto Rico — and a mother worth honoring.

Carlos, Alvaro, and Sebastian Ortega were born in Puerto Rico to Cuban parents before the family moved to Miami. In Puerto Rico, bodega culture runs deep — the kind of place where the person behind the counter knows your name. That's the feeling the brothers always wanted to build for themselves.

Their mother passed away in January 2025. Rather than a memorial, the brothers built something they could run together in her honor — a space built on the values she instilled in them. Family photos line the walls. In her memory, they also started the Te Amo Foundation, supporting mental health awareness.

They found the space almost by accident. Alvaro stopped by Fly Buy, the convenience store that used to sit at 2859 Bird Ave, and noticed the shelves nearly empty. The owner was already closing — and already had a buyer lined up. The brothers tracked him down anyway, asked for a chance to tell their story, and both the seller and the landlord said yes. They bought the property outright.
