About Dr. Browne

A Dietitian Who Grew Up
Eating From This Table.

Dr. Camille Browne grew up in Saint Lucia, witnessing family members struggle with diabetes and hypertension. After earning MSc in Clinical Dietetics from King's College London, she returned to help Caribbean people manage chronic disease without abandoning their food culture.

  • BSc Nutrition Sciences — UWI
  • MSc Clinical Dietetics — King's College London
  • Consultations in English & French Creole
Will I have to stop eating Caribbean food?
Absolutely not. Dr. Browne's approach is built around your cultural diet. You'll learn smarter portions and combinations — not elimination.
Do you work with PCOS clients?
Yes. PCOS and hormonal imbalance are central to Dr. Browne’s practice. She uses low-glycaemic, anti-inflammatory Caribbean food strategies to improve insulin sensitivity and cycle regularity.
