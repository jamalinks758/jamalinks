Background

Built on the Ground. Proven in the Field.

André St. Hill graduated from the University of the West Indies with a BEng in Civil Engineering before completing his MSc in Structural Engineering at the University of Manchester. Returning to Saint Lucia in 2010, he has since led structural design and site engineering on over 80 projects across 6 Eastern Caribbean islands.

His work spans residential foundations for private homes, multi-storey commercial steel-frame structures, infrastructure rehabilitation, and post-hurricane damage assessments — including Category 4 storm events in Dominica and Saint Martin. André works privately alongside his consultancy practice and accepts a selective number of new projects each quarter.

  • BEng Civil Engineering — University of the West Indies, St. Augustine
  • MSc Structural Engineering — University of Manchester, UK
  • Member of the Institution of Civil Engineers (MICE)
  • Eur Ing — European Engineering Designation (FEANI)
  • Registered Engineer — Saint Lucia Board of Engineers
  • BIM Level 2 Certified — Autodesk & BSI

Engagement Process

From Enquiry
to Certified Design

A structured process that protects both client and engineer — and delivers certifiable, permit-ready engineering documentation on time.

01

Initial Enquiry

Send project details on WhatsApp. Include location, building type, approximate size, and any existing drawings. André responds within one business day.

02

Site Assessment

An on-site visit to assess ground conditions, access, existing structures, and constraints. A formal fee proposal is issued within 5 working days of the visit.

03

Design & Calculations

Full structural analysis, member sizing, and hand-verified calculations. All work complies with Caribbean Regional Standards and UK Eurocodes where applicable.

04

Certified Drawings

Certified, stamped, and signed engineering drawings ready for planning authority submission, building permit applications, and contractor use.
