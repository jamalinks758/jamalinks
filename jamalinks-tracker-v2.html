⚡ Outreach Rules — Read Before Every Campaign

▼
▸Scout first. Always. Complete a full profile audit before any follow, like, or DM. Never approach blind.
▸Never cold-DM. Complete the full 7-day warm-up first. Minimum 5 days of engagement before DM 1. No exceptions.
▸Max 4 likes per session. Spaced across the day. Never mass-like a grid — it signals automation and destroys trust.
▸Comments must be post-specific. Reference the actual product, design, or detail. "Love this!" is noise. Noise does not build trust.
▸Never pitch in comments. Comments are trust deposits only. The DM is where business is done. Mixing the two destroys both.
▸Wait 3 full days before DM 2. One follow-up. One. Patience signals confidence. Desperation closes doors permanently.
▸Have a live demo ready before DM 3. Never pitch without proof. Walk in with it ready — not in progress.
▸5–7 days silence after DM = move-on trigger. Park them. Set a re-engage date. Never double-message after a follow-up.
▸Non-followers = message requests. DMs likely unread. Pivot to email or their listed contact channel instead.
