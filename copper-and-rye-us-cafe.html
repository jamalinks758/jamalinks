Our Story

Built for the
Early Risers.

Copper & Rye opened in 2018 with one obsession: make a cafe that Columbus actually deserves. Not a chain. Not a formula. A real kitchen and a real roaster under one roof.

We source our beans directly from three farms — one in Guatemala, one in Ethiopia, one in Colombia. Every bag is roasted in-house, 48 hours before it hits your cup.

The food menu changes seasonally. The commitment to not cutting corners doesn't.
