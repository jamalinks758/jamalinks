The Legacy

More Than a Bakery.
A Family Legacy.

The Velvet Buttercream is more than just a bakery — it's a family legacy. As a third-generation baker with over 20 years of experience and a culinary degree from the Art Institute of Phoenix, Aaron has honed his skills to ensure that every cake tells a story. Our commitment to quality and creativity allows us to design cakes that not only please the eye but also tantalize the palate.

Aaron advanced to The Art Institute of Phoenix where he earned his way onto the Dean's List for three consecutive semesters. Graduating in 2006, he worked as a Tournant Chef — learning every aspect of the kitchen before settling on his passion for working with sweets.

In 2010 he began laying the foundation for what is now The Velvet Buttercream. He is truly in it to change your perceptions by combining new modern with old classical. No stone has been left unturned, no cup of sugar left uncooked.

16 Years in the Making

🧁
The Signature Velvet Buttercream

A recipe 16 years in the making.

This 16-year-old recipe was created by Chef Aaron himself. The Signature Velvet Buttercream is a lightly sweetened, melt-in-your-mouth vanilla buttercream — designed like a traditional buttercream, as fluffy as whipped cream, and yet as velvety smooth and flavorful as a cream cheese icing.

Our cakes are built with 3 layers of cake and 2 layers of filling. For a taller, more sophisticated look, ask about our 5-layer "Tall-boy" premium option.
