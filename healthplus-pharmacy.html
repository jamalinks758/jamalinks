Our Pharmacist's Health Tips

Simple, practical advice that makes a real difference to your everyday health.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Dehydration worsens many health conditions and can affect how medications work.

Take Meds on Schedule

Set a daily alarm. Consistency matters — skipping doses or irregular timing can reduce how well your medication works.

Use Sunscreen Daily

UV rays in the Caribbean are intense year-round. SPF 30+ every day protects your skin from long-term damage.

Check Expiry Dates

Always check expiry dates before taking any medication. Bring expired meds to us — we'll dispose of them safely.
