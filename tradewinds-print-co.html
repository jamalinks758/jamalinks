Printing Materials

The right finish changes everything.

Matte

Feel: Soft, non-reflective

Best for: Business cards, brochures

Gloss

Feel: Reflective, vivid

Best for: Flyers, photo prints

Soft Touch

Feel: Velvety, premium

Best for: Packaging, invitations

Laminated

Feel: Durable, wipeable

Best for: Menus, signage

Recycled Paper

Feel: Natural, textured

Best for: Eco-conscious brands

Kraft

Feel: Rustic, sturdy

Best for: Packaging, tags

Foil Finish

Feel: Metallic, luxe

Best for: Wedding invites, premium cards

Spot UV

Feel: Glossy raised accents

Best for: Logos, standout details

Accepted Formats

CMYK vs RGB

CMYK — for print
RGB — for screens

Send files in CMYK where possible — RGB files are converted automatically but colors may shift slightly.

Bleed, Trim & Safe Area

Bleed Trim Safe Area

Keep text and logos inside the safe area — anything near the trim line risks being cut during finishing.
