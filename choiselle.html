The Origin

Born in a Kitchen.
Rooted in the
Caribbean.

"There is a place where nature is, well, natural."

— Panache Jamaica Magazine, on Choiselle

On the Caribbean island of Saint Lucia, in the shadows of the Pitons, surrounded by waterfalls and rainforests, Choiselle was born. Founded in 2014 by Nydia Norville — a Saint Lucian creator who began in her own kitchen — the brand started with a single mission: to formulate skincare that matches the nature of your skin with the nature around you.

What began with luxurious body butter grew into a full collection of water-free body oils, facial elixirs, and coconut wax candles — each crafted with certified organic ingredients, all-natural essential oil fragrances, and the botanical richness the Caribbean offers in abundance. Featured in four international publications and stocked across Saint Lucia and the United States, Choiselle remains rooted in the island that inspired it.

  • 2014
    Founded by Nydia Norville. Launched with luxurious body butter. Made in Saint Lucia.
  • 2015
    Featured in Beauty Store Business Magazine (USA) — Lemongrass Oil named an upmarket offering.
  • 2017
    Expanded into bath & body oils and coconut wax candles. International media features follow.
  • Now
    Three sister brands. Six-plus retail stockists. Island-wide delivery. Growing globally.
