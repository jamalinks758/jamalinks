The Player

Built on a Concrete Court.
Reading the Whole Floor.

Tiana Prescott grew up playing on the outdoor courts of Castries, Saint Lucia — often the only girl, always the smallest on the floor. What she lacked in height, she made up for with an instinct for the game that coaches noticed by age 12: she didn't just play basketball, she read it.

At 22, she leads the Saint Lucia National Team in assists, was named MVP of the 2024 Caribbean Basketball Championship, and has drawn interest from collegiate programs and professional scouts across two continents. Her game is built on vision, pace, and a court IQ that has become her defining trait.

Off the court, Tiana represents something larger than her own career — she is one of the first Caribbean women's basketball players to build a genuine international following, opening a pathway that very few before her have had access to.
