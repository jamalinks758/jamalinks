Our Ministry

A Church Built on
Faith, Community & Purpose

Living Word Ministry is a Spirit-filled congregation rooted in Biblical truth, committed to building strong families, reaching the community of Saint Lucia, and raising disciples who walk in the fullness of God's calling.

Worship

Encounter God through passionate, Spirit-led worship every Sunday. Our services are a space where every heart can be renewed and every burden lifted.

Teaching

Grounded, practical, Word-based teaching that equips believers for everyday life. Wednesday Bible Study and Sunday messages available online.

Community

About the Pastor

A Servant Leader
Rooted in Saint Lucia

Elijah Fontaine was born and raised in Saint Lucia, the second of five children in a household where faith was not a Sunday habit but a daily way of life. He answered his calling to ministry at the age of 24, having spent his early years as a teacher — an experience that would permanently shape how he communicates the Word: clearly, practically, and with the whole person in mind.

Over 22 years of pastoral ministry, he has built Living Word Ministry from a small home prayer group into a congregation of over 500 members, with a reputation across Saint Lucia for accessible Biblical teaching, compassionate community outreach, and a willingness to speak plainly into the real challenges that Caribbean families face.

His ministry extends beyond the church walls — through prison visits, hospital chaplaincy, school programmes, and a growing online audience following his weekly sermon series. He is, above all, a pastor who remembers where he came from and who he is called to serve.

  • Bachelor of Theology — Caribbean Evangelical Theological Association
  • 22 years in active pastoral ministry, Saint Lucia
  • Founded Living Word Ministry — 500+ congregation
  • Prison chaplaincy, hospital ministry, school outreach
  • Weekly sermons available online — Caribbean & diaspora reach
