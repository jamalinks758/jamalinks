About Krishna

Caribbean Life,
Told Beautifully.

"She doesn't just visit Saint Lucia — she interprets it. Every reel, every post, every caption is a love letter to the island that raised her."

From the Pitons to Sugar Beach arriving by boat, outdoor villa baths at The Wooden House, kayaking the turquoise coast, Jazz Festival nights lit golden, and finding the best hidden food spots the island keeps to itself — Krishna Henry's content is lived experience, not performance. Her audience trusts her because she earns it, daily, post by post.

With a blue-verified Instagram presence, 63K TikTok followers, 2M+ likes, and active channels on YouTube and Facebook, she has built one of the Caribbean's most authentic creator platforms — and one of the most brand-ready.
